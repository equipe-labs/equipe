# The Mac's desktop apps

Look first: `screenshot`, then `elements` for the app's buttons and fields — clicking or typing into an element id is
steadier than coordinates, which are pixels of your last screenshot. `open_app` or `focus_window` says which app you
mean. A click is refused when the point isn't in that app, and typing when the keyboard focus isn't: look again and
bring the app forward rather than retrying blindly, and stop if the person seems to be using the Mac. Check what you
typed in the reply or with `elements`.
