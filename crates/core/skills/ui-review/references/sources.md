# Sources of the `ui-*` skills

The `ui-*` skills are equipe's own writing. They draw on the public sources below, summarised in our own words with
nothing quoted beyond a short phrase. Where a source's structure or rules were adapted, its licence is named; the MIT
notices are kept in the repository's `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `licenses/`.

## Adapted (MIT, with attribution)

- **Vercel Web Interface Guidelines** — https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines — MIT, Copyright (c)
  2025 Vercel Labs. Rules on focus, hit targets, loading-state timing, optimistic updates, URL state, forms, motion
  properties, layout stability and content details inform `ui-polish`, `ui-forms`, `ui-motion`, `ui-accessibility`,
  `ui-foundations` and `ui-writing`.
- **Mobbin skills** — https://github.com/mobbin/skills — MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 Mobbin. The `redesign-screen`
  skill's job walk, its cap on changes per pass and its rules for placing a new part (copy the nearest sibling, one
  thing leads, use the lines already there, change the least) are adapted in `ui-review` and
  `ui-layout-and-states`.

## Read and summarised

- **WCAG 2.2** and its Understanding documents — https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/ (W3C). Success criteria cited by
  number in `ui-accessibility` and elsewhere.
- **WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices** — https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/ (W3C).
- **web.dev** — https://web.dev/articles/vitals — Core Web Vitals thresholds (LCP, INP, CLS at the 75th percentile).
- **Nielsen Norman Group** — https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ — response-time limits, usability heuristics,
  progressive disclosure, empty states, forms, data tables, microcopy.
- **GOV.UK Design System** — https://design-system.service.gov.uk — question pages and error messages; content under
  the Open Government Licence v3.0.
- **Apple Human Interface Guidelines** — https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines — platform
  navigation, target sizes, text styles.
- **Material Design 3** — https://m3.material.io — Android navigation, target sizes, motion.
- **IBM Carbon** — https://carbondesignsystem.com — data table sizes and dense-tool patterns.
- **GitHub Primer** — https://primer.style; **Shopify Polaris** — https://polaris-react.shopify.com; **Atlassian
  Design System** — https://atlassian.design; **Vercel Geist** — https://vercel.com/geist — dense-tool layouts,
  tables, filters, messages and content guidance.
- **Emil Kowalski** — https://emilkowal.ski — free articles on animation craft (durations, easing, press feedback,
  origin-aware popovers, tooltip delays).
- **Rauno Freiberg** — https://rauno.me — writing on interaction design (selective animation, direct manipulation,
  interruptibility).
- **Refactoring UI** (Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger) — principles as they are commonly summarised in public (space
  before borders, hierarchy through weight and colour, starting roomy); the book itself is not reproduced.

Product names used as reference families (Linear, Stripe, Mercury, Vercel, Notion, Raycast, GitHub) point at public
products to study for structure and proportion — never at their brands, assets or words.
