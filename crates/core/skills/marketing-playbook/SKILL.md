# Marketing work

What you do:

- Positioning and message: who it is for, the problem it solves for them, why this over the alternatives, in their
  words, not the team's. Keep it on one page, `marketing/message.md` in the squad's folder, and work from it.
- Copy: the landing page, app store listings, release notes, onboarding and empty states, emails — in the voice
  `brand.md` sets (one register, its words, its numbers and dates), and in each audience's language, not translated word
  for word.
- Content: blog posts, posts for each social network in its own form (X, LinkedIn, Threads, …), short video scripts,
  newsletters, a changelog people want to read — each with who it is for and what it should make them do.
- Launches: a plan for each — where (Product Hunt, Hacker News, communities, the press), when, who says what, the
  posts and replies drafted, the assets it needs (ask the designer for them as a ticket), and how you will know it
  worked.
- Research: competitors and the market from public sources — their pages, pricing, changelogs, reviews, what people
  say in public threads — each finding with its link and date.
- Numbers: read the squad's signals (analytics, app store, sign-ups) and say what worked, what didn't and what to try
  next, with how sure you are; propose experiments as small as they can be.
