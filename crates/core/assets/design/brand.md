# Brand

> Fill this in before any colour or typeface. Each section ends in a decision the
> person made — taste is theirs; write what they chose and why.

## 1. Who it is for
- Who uses it, in one line (e.g. 투자 심사역과 펀드 운용 담당자).
- What they come to do, and what they fear getting wrong.

## 2. The brand in words — this, not that
Three to five attributes, each with what it is *not* (this, not that):

| Attribute | This | Not that |
|---|---|---|
| e.g. 신뢰 | 숫자가 또렷하고 출처가 보인다 | 딱딱하고 차갑다 |
|  |  |  |
|  |  |  |

## 3. References
Two or three things the person admires (a link each, and what to take from it),
and one thing it must not look like.

## 4. What exists already
Logo, colours, type, voice found on the current site, deck or app — from
`equipe design audit <url>` (`audit/brand-audit.png`). Mark each **brand** (kept)
or **accident** (one-off).

## 5. Voice and tone
- Register: 해요체 or 합니다체 — one, everywhere (buttons and errors too).
- Words it uses / words it avoids (e.g. 「확인하기」, not 「컨펌」).
- Numbers and units: how money, dates and percentages are written (e.g. 12,500백만원, 2026-10-19).

Sample lines, in the voice:

| Where | Line |
|---|---|
| Primary button | 저장하기 |
| Empty state | 아직 기록이 없어요. 첫 투자를 추가해 보세요. |
| Error | 사업자 번호는 숫자 10자리예요. 다시 확인해 주세요. |
| Success | 저장했어요. |
| Destructive confirm | 이 펀드를 삭제할까요? 되돌릴 수 없어요. |

## 6. Brief (binding, before any option)
- One specific reference — a named product, site or print piece — and what to take from it.
- Visual thesis, in one line.
- Locked axes (kept as they are) and free axes (to explore).

## 7. Locked
The brand's own values — kept, and not flagged by `equipe design check` as clichés.
List each with where it comes from (e.g. `#C1262D` logo red · from the audit; Proxima Nova · site headings).

## 8. Decisions
Dated, one line each: what was chosen, from which options, why. The person's taste goes in `taste.md`.
